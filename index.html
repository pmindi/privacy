Privacy Policy

Application: Crime Factory Uploader · Last updated: 15 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Crime Factory Uploader application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when it accesses Google and YouTube services on behalf of its sole operator. The App is a personal, automated tool used by its owner to upload videos to, and read analytics from, the owner's own YouTube channel.

1. Who operates this App

The App is operated privately by an individual creator. It is not a public product and is not offered to other users. Contact: mindipraveen031@gmail.com.

2. What data the App accesses

When authorized, the App uses the following Google API scopes, solely against the operator's own Google/YouTube account:

  • youtube.upload — upload video files to the operator's channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl — manage the operator's own videos, metadata, and comments.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — read view, watch-time, and retention statistics for the operator's channel.
  • yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — read monetary/revenue statistics for the operator's channel.

The App accesses only the operator's own account data. It does not access, request, or process data belonging to any other person.

3. How the data is used

Data retrieved via these scopes is used exclusively to (a) publish the operator's own videos on a schedule and (b) analyze the performance of the operator's own videos to guide future content. All processing happens locally on the operator's machine.

4. Data storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and analytics results are stored locally on the operator's own computer. The App does not transmit, sell, share, or disclose any Google user data to third parties. No data is served to any external users, because the App has no external users.

5. Limited Use disclosure

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide the App's function to its operator, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored tokens and analytics can be deleted by the operator at any time by removing them from the local machine and revoking the App's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App evolves. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
